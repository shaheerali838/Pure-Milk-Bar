## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, operational mastery, and uncompromising reliability. Built for an on-demand marketplace bridging homeowners and elite trade specialists, the aesthetic balances modern technological precision with the tactile authenticity of skilled physical trades. 

The style sits at the intersection of **Architectural Minimalism** and **Editorial Utility**:
- Confident, deliberate typography inspired by high-end trade catalogues and contemporary architectural monographs.
- Elimination of visual clutter, drop shadows, and noisy micro-interactions in favor of hairline structural grids, crisp negative space, and functional hierarchy.
- A tone that feels calm, transparent, and dignified—treating trade professionals as masters of their craft rather than commoditized gig labor.

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical clarity paired with organic depth. 

- **Primary (`#0F766E`) & Primary Dark (`#115E59`)**: Grounded deep teal acting as the signature of trust, verification, and technical mastery. Used for primary calls to action, brand touchpoints, and verified badges.
- **Primary Light (`#CCFBF1`)**: High-luminance mint wash reserved for state accents, active pill toggles, and contextual badge highlights.
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`) & Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure slate-white base providing high contrast against structural hairline boundaries.
- **Text Main (`#0F172A`) & Text Secondary (`#64748B`)**: Deep ink slate for primary typographic weights; cool slate for supporting specifications, metadata, and timestamps.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`)**: A disciplined 1px hairline framework that structures content without relying on drop shadows.
- **Semantic Accents**: Success (`#16A34A`), Warning (`#F59E0B`), and Error (`#DC2626`) are calibrated for immediate scannability across booking updates and service validation.

## Typography

The type system uses `Inter` configured with tight, negative tracking in display tiers and neutral tracking across micro-copy. 

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in Semi-Bold (`600`) with tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) to create an authoritative, editorial density reminiscent of Swiss modernist publishing.
- **Body**: Uses Regular (`400`) at comfortable line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless legibility for complex scope-of-work documentation and diagnostic checklists.
- **Labels & Metadata**: `label-sm` leverages uppercase styling with expanded letter-spacing (`+0.04em`) to designate status tags, service tier categories, and verified credentials clearly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt architectural rhythm, utilizing asymmetric editorial compositions rather than modular repetitive grids.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column desktop grid with a max-width of `1200px` (`75rem`), 24px gutters, and 32px page margins. Tablet collapses to 8 columns with 20px gutters; mobile adapts to a single-column or 4-column flow with 16px lateral padding.
- **Section Rhythm**: Broad vertical intervals (`4.5rem` to `6rem` on desktop, `2.5rem` to `3rem` on mobile) create distinct pauses between discovery tiers, expert profiles, and pricing calculators.
- **Asymmetric Composition**: Instead of uniform card walls, layouts prioritize variable-width horizontal bands, split-column service overviews (40% sticky specification index / 60% flowing detail panel), and anchored summary ledgers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy raster shadows, diffuse ambient multi-layered blurs, and layered skeuomorphic depths. Spatial relationships are defined strictly through **Low-Contrast Outlines** and **Tonal Planar Stacking**.

- **Planar Tiers**:
  - `Base`: Background canvas (`#F8FAFC`).
  - `Surface 1`: Elevated modules, action rows, and inputs set in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
  - `Surface 2`: Hover states, flyouts, and modal drawers sitting on white (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`#CBD5E1`) and a singular crisp drop: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Structural Lines**: Subtle hairline dividers (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) separate line items, timelines, and technical summaries, anchoring content cleanly without physical elevation.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary is understated and disciplined (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Containers & Modules**: Subtle corner radii (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) lend an engineered precision reminiscent of industrial blueprints and modern hardware.
- **Interactive Controls**: Buttons, text inputs, and table rows use an exact `0.375rem` (6px) or `0.5rem` (8px) radius to maintain crisp boundary lines.
- **Exception (Status Badges)**: Status pills and verification indicators use pill radii (`9999px`) to immediately separate qualitative meta-tags from structural interactive panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Teal (`#0F766E`) background, white (`#FFFFFF`) text, 0.375rem radius, zero shadow. Hover shifts to Dark Teal (`#115E59`); active state applies subtle scale compression (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Outline**: White background, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, text in `#0F172A`. Hover deepens border to `#0F766E` with text shifting to `#0F766E`.
- **Ghost / Minimal**: No background or border; `#0F766E` text with medium weight and an inline contextual chevron for forward motion.

### Input Fields
- White fill, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, 0.375rem radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus shifts border to `#0F766E` with a hairline `0 0 0 1px #0F766E` inner ring. No glowing halos. Placeholder text styled in `#94A3B8`.

### Verification Badges & Chips
- Filter chips sit on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Selected state switches background to `#CCFBF1`, border to `#0F766E`, and label to `#0F766E`.
- The "Verified Master" badge is rendered with a `#CCFBF1` background, `#0F766E` icon and text, formatted in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Cards & Service Rows
- Eliminate repetitive elevated box cards. Instead, use connected list registries and segmented tabular rows.
- Each service category is expressed as a full-bleed hairline row (`1px border-b #E2E8F0`) with generous vertical padding (`1.5rem`), left-aligned service craft nomenclature, fixed price-transparency indices, and an expand/book trigger.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Custom square controls with 3px border radius. Unselected uses a 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border; selected displays a solid `#0F766E` fill with an optically centered sharp white checkmark.

### Booking Ledgers (Marketplace Specific)
- Floating summary panels anchored at the right margin or bottom viewport, featuring tabular numerical alignments, transparent fee breakdowns, and SLA guarantee statements set in `mono-spec` typography.
